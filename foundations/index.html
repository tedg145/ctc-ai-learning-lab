Core concept

Foundations

This page explains the central mental model behind the lab: large language models predict likely next tokens based on patterns in language and context. That gives them power, but it also explains why verification matters.

1

Prediction, not magic

LLMs generate language by estimating what token is most likely to come next based on the context. Fluent output is not the same as guaranteed truth.

2

Context matters

Better prompts reduce ambiguity. Audience, source material, constraints, and purpose help the model narrow the possible response space.

3

Verification is part of the job

Use AI to think, draft, and explore. Then verify for correctness, tone, policy alignment, and suitability before acting.
